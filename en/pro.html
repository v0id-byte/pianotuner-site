PRO FEATURES

Six exclusive Pro features

Tailored for professional tuners and audiophile piano owners.

01 · OPTIMIZER

On-device stretch optimizer

Fits a bespoke stretch curve on-device from measured inharmonicity (B) — deterministic, reproducible.

02 · ANALYSIS

Professional frequency analysis

Acoustic-based frequency analysis that holds the target across the top octaves, and across the full range.

03 · TURBO

Turbo performance mode

Unlocks motor speed limits for faster response, drastically reducing total 88-key tuning time.

Auto-syncs tuning data to create digital customer archives, recording historical piano states.
